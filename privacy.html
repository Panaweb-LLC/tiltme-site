TiltMe Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

TiltMe is published by Panaweb LLC ("we"). This policy explains what the TiltMe Android app does with your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. TiltMe has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. Your scores, settings, and game progress are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Motion sensors

TiltMe reads your device's accelerometer so you can steer by tilting. The readings are used only in the moment to control the game. They are not saved or sent anywhere.

Purchases

The optional "Never Wait Again" upgrade is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment under the Google Privacy Policy. We never see your payment details. The app asks Google Play only whether you own the upgrade.

Children

TiltMe is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
